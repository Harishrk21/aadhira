# Off-Page SEO Execution Playbook

This playbook is the practical system to improve authority signals that cannot be solved by code alone.

## Targets (90-day)

- Increase referring domains by 40+
- Build 25+ high-quality dofollow links
- Build 40+ structured citations (local + healthcare)
- Keep anchor profile diversified (avoid exact-match overuse)

## Anchor Strategy

Use a balanced distribution:

- 45% branded anchors (`Arura Integral Therapy Services`, `Arura`)
- 30% partial-match anchors (`speech therapy in Chennai`, `occupational therapy for kids`)
- 15% generic anchors (`learn more`, `this resource`)
- 10% exact-match anchors (`best occupational therapy in Chennai`)

## Priority Linkable Assets On Site

- `/best-occupational-therapy-in-chennai`
- `/best-speech-therapy-for-kids-in-chennai`
- `/best-aba-therapy-in-chennai`
- `/glossary`
- High-value blog and location pages

## Weekly Workflow

1. Publish one high-intent page (glossary, service-location, or condition-therapy page).
2. Outreach to 30 prospects (health blogs, parenting media, local Chennai business media).
3. Submit 10 citations (Google Business Profile, local directories, healthcare listings).
4. Acquire at least 3 contextual links from relevant pages.
5. Log every action in tracker with URL, anchor text, status, and date.

## Link Quality Rules

- Prefer topical relevance over raw domain metrics.
- Avoid spammy PBNs, spun content directories, and paid link farms.
- Ensure each backlink points to a page with strong internal links and clear conversion CTA.

## Citation Consistency (NAP)

Keep these exactly consistent across all listings:

- Brand: Arura Integral Therapy Services
- Website: https://aruratherapy.in
- Email: info.dunmark@gmail.com
- Phones: 6379262127, 7200766120, 7200766143, 7200766720
- City focus: Chennai (Villivakkam and nearby areas)

## Reporting Dashboard (weekly)

Track:

- new referring domains
- new backlinks (dofollow vs nofollow)
- top linked pages
- anchor distribution
- rankings for top 20 commercial keywords
- leads generated from organic traffic

## Notes

Off-page SEO scores and authority are gradual. Consistency over 3-6 months gives compounding gains.
